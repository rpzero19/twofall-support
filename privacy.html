Twofall Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

The short version

Twofall does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

What Twofall stores

Your game in progress, your scores and statistics, and your sound and haptics settings. This is saved only in the app's private storage on your iPhone.

What Twofall does not do

There is no account and no sign-in. Twofall has no servers, and contains no advertising, analytics, in-app purchases or third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites. The game works fully offline and makes no network requests. The only links that leave the app are the support and privacy pages in Settings, which open in your browser when you tap them.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, your Twofall data may be included in that backup under Apple's terms. Twofall itself never receives it.

Deleting your data

Reset your statistics in the game's Settings, or delete the app to remove everything Twofall has stored.

Children

Twofall collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page. Questions: ios.apps.build@gmail.com
