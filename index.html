Twofall

A number merge puzzle for iPhone. Drop, merge, double.

Common questions

How do I play? Tap a column to drop the tile, or drag left and right to aim and let go. Tiles with the same number that touch merge into one tile of double the value. The full guide is in the game under the question mark button.

What does the hammer do? It removes any one tile you choose. You start each game with one and earn another every time you make a new biggest tile, from 128 up. You can hold three.

Why did my small tiles disappear? Once you build very large tiles, the smallest numbers stop appearing and any left on the board are cleared for you.

What is the daily challenge? Every day has one fixed order of tiles, the same for every player. You can replay it as often as you like; your best score for the day is kept.

Is my game saved? Yes, after every move. Close the app at any time and pick Continue when you come back.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. Twofall has no account, no ads and no servers. See the privacy policy.
